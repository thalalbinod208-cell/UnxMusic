UnX Music is an open-source Android music app based on SimpMusic. Browse and play music using YouTube Music data, build your library, and listen with a player designed for Android.

<br><b>Highlights:</b>

- Browse music, charts, podcasts, moods, and genres
- Search for songs, artists, albums, and videos
- Play music in the background and control playback from Android media controls
- Save favorites, organize playlists, and view listening history and analytics
- Cache supported tracks for offline playback
- View synced lyrics from available providers
- Customize the app appearance and playback settings
- Use Android Auto where supported

UnX Music is an independent community distribution based on the open-source SimpMusic project. It is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Spotify, Discord, or the upstream project maintainer. Online catalog access and some integrations depend on third-party services and may change or be unavailable.
